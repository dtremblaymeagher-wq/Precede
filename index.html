Predictive Roadmap — with real confidence scores

Précède pulls your actual Jira velocity — carry-over rate, story point completion, sprint history — and computes a best/likely/worst delivery window for every epic. Not a Gantt chart. A probability model.

Example: "Epic 'Checkout v2' — 68% confidence to ship by June 30"

Signal Radar — from noise to decision in seconds

Paste a customer call transcript, a Slack thread, a Gong note. The Radar extracts emerging trends, scores OKR alignment, flags what's untracked in your backlog, and measures how fast your team converts signals into shipped stories.

Tracks lead time: signal captured → story shipped

Untracked Demand — what your backlog is ignoring

Précède cross-references your customer signals against your active backlog. It surfaces recurring themes that have no story attached — so real demand doesn't quietly disappear between sprint planning sessions.

Persists across analyses — nothing falls through the cracks

Every sprint story is scored against your OKRs. Précède calculates how many story points are actively serving each objective — and flags strategic gaps before your next exec review, not after.
